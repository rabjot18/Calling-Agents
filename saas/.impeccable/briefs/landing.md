# Surface brief: / (marketing landing) — mode: Persuade

Audience: owners of small appointment businesses (salons, clinics, restaurants, trades) who miss calls while working.
Action: Set up your receptionist (→ /signup → onboarding). Proof: the product at work — live day page, a real-sounding sample call, real screens with sample data.
Constraints: no invented prices, customers, testimonials or metrics; sample data labelled; Outlook/WhatsApp not claimed (not live).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The phone rings and the day page writes itself. Refuses the category default: purple gradient, split hero with chat bubbles over a "trusted by" row.
OWN-WORLD: Bound appointment book. Book-cloth green #1d3a30 drenches hero and close with a linen grain; paper #fbfcfa/#fff ruled sheets for proof sections; ink #16201b; pen blue #24477a for callers and annotations; ribbon red #b3261e only for urgent. Archivo condensed 800 display, Archivo body. Hour-ruled rows, tags, the ribbon, the Pulse bars mark.
STORY: Visitor sees a call become a written entry, hears Maya book a caller and spell the name back, sees the real day page, learns setup is 10 minutes, finds their trade, trusts the rules, signs up.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Cloth ground. Left 5/12: three-line condensed headline ~96px, one line of sub, paper "Set up your receptionist" + "Hear a 25-second call". Right 7/12: tilted paper day page (9am–2pm) with red ribbon, being filled live by an incoming-call strip and transcript lines.
FORM: Signature interaction = live day-page writer (hero) + transcript-synced call player. seed: none (unavailable) — launcher not installed; user granted full creative freedom.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
