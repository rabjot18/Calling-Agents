# Luxe Hair Studio: three test calls

### 1. Booking a colour appointment (web page)
You are: a new client who isn't sure what to ask for.
Say: "Hi, I'd like to book some colour."
Then: when asked which kind, say "balayage". When asked about previous colour, say "box dye about three months ago". Ask for Michael, Saturday morning.
It should:
- Ask which kind of colour, and about treatments in the last six months.
- Mention a consultation or patch test may be needed.
- Quote balayage as starting at $220 plus tax, about 3.5 hours (if you ask).
- Today (request mode): take your name, number and preferred time, and say the team will confirm. Once Cal.com is connected: offer up to three real times and book after you say yes.

Red flags: an invented price, a promised time it didn't book, or not asking which colour.

### 2. Insisting on a person (use the phone number once it's attached; transfers don't work on the web page)
You are: an unhappy client calling during opening hours (Mon–Sat, 9–6 Atlantic).
Say: "I need to speak to someone about my appointment yesterday."
Then: decline the offer of a message twice, saying "No, I want a real person."
It should:
- Offer to help or take a message first.
- Only after you've insisted two or three times, say "I'll connect you now" and ring +1 782-***-****, where you'll hear a short whisper about who's calling.
- Outside opening hours: no transfer, and it takes a message instead.

Red flags: transferring on the first ask, or transferring on a Sunday.

### 3. After-hours message (web page; call on a Sunday or after 6 pm Atlantic)
You are: a client wanting a gift card.
Say: "Hi, are you open? I wanted to ask about gift cards."
It should:
- Say the salon is closed and reopens Monday (or tomorrow) at 9 am.
- Offer to take a message, collect your name and number and read the number back, and mention the team will call back.
- Not invent gift card prices or policies.

Red flags: saying it's open, guessing gift card details, or no message taken.
