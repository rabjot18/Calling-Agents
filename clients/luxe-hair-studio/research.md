2. **Answers 24/7.** During hours it handles everything and offers to take a message for staff. After hours it still books, reschedules and answers questions, and takes messages for the next opening.
3. **Hours** (if not given): Tue–Fri 9 am–7 pm, Sat 9 am–5 pm, closed Sun–Mon. A typical Long Island salon schedule, **flagged as an assumption** (question 1).
4. **No live transfer** until the owner gives a number (question 3). "Speak to someone" leads to an urgent message.
5. **Consultation-first services:** color correction, extensions and bridal. The agent books a consultation, not the service.
6. **Color callers:** the agent clarifies the exact service and asks about color or chemical treatments in the last 6 months. New color clients are told the stylist may want a quick patch test or consultation.
7. **Cancellation policy:** 24 hours' notice. The agent states it but never charges or waives fees.
8. **Prices:** quote only prices the owner provides (or ones in Cal.com event descriptions), as "starting at". Otherwise the stylist gives an exact quote.
9. **Booking details:** name, mobile number, email (Cal.com needs one for the booking), service, stylist preference, time.
10. **Messages:** captured on every call and visible in the Retell dashboard. Delivery by email or text needs the owner's address (question 4) and a one-time setup (see REPORT).
11. **Recording/AI disclosure** in the greeting.
12. **Language:** English (US).
